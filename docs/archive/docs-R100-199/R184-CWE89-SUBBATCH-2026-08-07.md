## R184 — Engine change

No engine change. R184 is a **process change**: re-run the full CWE-89 family (3682 files across s01/s02/s03/s04) with the sub-batching strategy from `juliet-eval-sub.ps1`, but applied at the `s0X` sub-folder granularity instead of per-flow.

### Why sub-batching

CWE-89 in merged mode is O(N²) for the cross-class taint analysis. The full 3682-file run timed out at 30 minutes in R180c and R181. Splitting by sub-folder gives 4 batches of ~700-980 files each, which runs in ~120-180s per batch (total ~10 minutes).

## Lessons

1. **Sub-batching is a free 4× speedup** for the cross-class taint analysis. The merged-mode O(N²) is dominated by the cross-class call graph, not by the per-class work. Splitting at the sub-folder boundary keeps the cross-class calls within each batch (the boundary matches Juliet's source-sink template grouping).
2. **The R182 bridge has a 8.5% recall payoff on CWE-89** because CWE-89 s01-s04 all use the `Integer.parseInt(request.getParameter("id"))` → SQL sink pattern. This is the largest single-round recall gain so far.
3. **First full measurement of CWE-89**: 35.7% recall, 815 TP, 0 FP. The remaining 1 465 FN are dominated by the `_61b`/`_66b`/`_67b` helper files where the entry point's taint should propagate but doesn't yet.
